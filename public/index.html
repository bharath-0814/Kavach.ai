💡 What Problem is Kavach Solving & How?

🎯

The Problem in India

Over 80% of Indian SMS phishing scams exploit Latin-script Hindi (Hinglish) and regional languages (e.g. "Aapka bijli cut ho jayega", "khata bandh"). Standard security models are trained on English text and completely miss phonetic variations, intentional leetspeak, and regional urgency coercion.

🔬

Fuzzy Levenshtein Engine

Attackers intentionally bypass keyword filters by writing BLCK, 0TPP, K-Y-C, or b4nd. Kavach's deobfuscation pipeline normalizes leetspeak and uses Levenshtein distance ($\le 2$) matching against an Indian threat lexicon without causing false positives on natural Hindi chat.

🧠

AI Fusion Layer

Combines the deterministic speed of our vernacular regex rule engine (40%) with the multilingual reasoning power of Google Gemini Flash (60%). It operates with automatic retry exponential backoff and graceful heuristic fallback.
