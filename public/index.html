💡 The Kavach Defense Architecture

🎯

The Vernacular Blindspot

Over 80% of Indian smishing scams use transliterated Hindi (Hinglish) and regional languages ("bijli disconnect", "khata bandh") packed with leetspeak ("BLCK", "0TPP"). Standard security tools miss these entirely.

⚡

Sub-50ms Levenshtein Engine

Our deterministic pre-processor normalizes leetspeak, collapses spaced single-character tokens ("K-Y-C"), and computes length-adaptive edit distance against a 200+ Indian threat lexicon without distorting genuine words.

🧠

Multi-Model AI Fusion Layer

Blends deterministic regex rule scoring (40%) with Google Gemini Flash (60%) with instant multi-model failover pool (Gemini 3.5 Flash, 3.7 Flash, Flash Latest) for uncompromised resilience.
